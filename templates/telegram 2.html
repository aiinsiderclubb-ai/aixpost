How to Connect Telegram Bot
Step 1: Create a Bot

Start a chat with @BotFather on Telegram and create your bot using the /newbot command.

Step 2: Get Your Chat ID

Send any message to your bot, then visit:
https://api.telegram.org/bot<BOT_TOKEN>/getUpdates
Look for the chat.id number in the response.

## 1. Fetching Groups {#fetch}

### How does group fetching work?

AIPostX automatically discovers all Facebook groups you're a member of by securely logging into your account and scanning your group memberships. This process is completely automated and typically takes 2-5 minutes.

### The Process:

1. **Secure Login:** Your credentials are encrypted and stored locally
2. **Group Discovery:** We scan your Facebook profile for group memberships
3. **Data Collection:** Group names, URLs, member counts, and activity levels are saved
4. **Organization:** Groups are categorized by language and activity for easy management

### Quick Start:
1. Go to Dashboard
2. Enter your Facebook credentials
3. Click "Start Fetching"
4. Wait for the process to complete

### Common Questions:

**Q: How long does fetching take?**
A: Typically 2-5 minutes depending on how many groups you're in. Large accounts (500+ groups) may take up to 10 minutes.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! Your credentials are encrypted using military-grade encryption and stored locally. We never store your password on our servers.

**Q: What if I get a CAPTCHA?**
A: Turn off headless mode and solve the CAPTCHA manually when prompted. The process will continue automatically after solving.

**Q: Can I update my groups later?**
A: Yes, you can re-run the fetching process at any time to update your group list.

## 2. Creating Post Templates {#post}

### How to create effective posts?

AIPostX provides a powerful templating system that allows you to create dynamic, personalized messages for each group while maintaining a natural, human-like appearance.

### Template Variables:

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `{{group_name}}` | Name of the target group | "Digital Marketing Pro" |
| `{{random_greeting}}` | Random greeting phrase | "Hello everyone" |
| `{{random_emoji}}` | Random relevant emoji | "🚀" |
| `{{time_based}}` | Time-based greeting | "Good morning" |
| `{{user_name}}` | Your name | "John" |
| `{{random_transition}}` | Random transition phrase | "By the way" |

### Example Template:
```
{{random_greeting}} {{group_name}}! 🚀

I wanted to share an amazing opportunity that I think would be perfect for this community.

{{random_transition}}, I've been working on a new project that helps people automate their social media presence, and I'd love to get your thoughts on it.

{{random_emoji}} What do you think about automation tools for social media?

Best regards,
{{user_name}}
```

### Best Practices:

- **Keep it personal:** Use variables to reference the group name
- **Be authentic:** Write in your natural voice and style
- **Add value:** Share useful information, not just promotional content
- **Use emojis sparingly:** 1-2 emojis per message for engagement
- **Test first:** Always preview your templates before mass posting
- **Vary your content:** Create multiple templates to avoid repetition
- **Follow group rules:** Respect each group's posting guidelines

## 3. Scheduling & Automation {#schedule}

### How does scheduling work?

Schedule your campaigns to run at optimal times for maximum engagement. Our intelligent scheduling system respects platform limits and ensures your posts appear natural.

### Scheduling Options:

#### Smart Delays
- Random delays between posts (10-60 seconds)
- Appears human-like to avoid detection
- Prevents spam flags

#### Scheduled Jobs
- Set up recurring campaigns
- Daily, weekly, or monthly posting
- Automatic execution at optimal times

### Optimal Posting Times:

**Weekdays:**
- 9-11 AM: Peak morning engagement
- 2-4 PM: Afternoon activity spike
- 7-9 PM: Evening social browsing

**Weekends:**
- 10 AM - 2 PM: Weekend leisure browsing
- 8-10 PM: Weekend evening activity

### Rate Limiting Guidelines:

- **Maximum:** 20-30 posts per hour for safety
- **Recommended:** 10-15 posts per hour
- **Breaks:** Take 30-60 minute breaks between sessions
- **Daily Limit:** 100-150 posts maximum per day

### Advanced Scheduling:

1. **Time Zone Targeting:** Schedule posts based on group's local time
2. **Day-of-Week Optimization:** Different schedules for different days
3. **Seasonal Adjustments:** Adapt timing based on holidays and events
4. **A/B Testing:** Test different times to find optimal windows

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:

**Issue: Posts are being marked as spam**
- Solution: Increase delays between posts, vary content more, use fewer promotional messages

**Issue: Low engagement rates**
- Solution: Post at optimal times, improve content quality, target more active groups

**Issue: Account restrictions**
- Solution: Reduce posting frequency, take breaks, ensure content follows platform guidelines

**Issue: Groups not loading**
- Solution: Check internet connection, verify credentials, try fetching again

### Best Practices Summary:

1. **Start Small:** Begin with 5-10 groups to test your approach
2. **Monitor Performance:** Check analytics regularly and adjust strategy
3. **Follow Guidelines:** Respect platform rules and group policies
4. **Be Patient:** Building engagement takes time and consistency
5. **Stay Updated:** Keep up with platform changes and best practices

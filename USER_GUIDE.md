# 🚀 Facebook SaaS Platform - User Guide

## Quick Start Guide for Facebook Group Posting Automation

### 🌟 **Platform URL:** http://localhost:8080

---

## 📋 STEP 1: Registration & Login

### **New Users:**
1. 🌐 Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser
2. 📝 Fill the **Registration Form**:
   - Email address
   - First & Last name 
   - Strong password (min 8 chars, uppercase, lowercase, number, special char)
3. ✅ Click **"Create Account"**
4. 🎉 You're automatically logged in!

### **Existing Users:**
1. 🌐 Open http://localhost:8080
2. 🔐 Use the **Login Form** with your credentials
3. ✅ Click **"Sign In"**

---

## 🚀 STEP 2: Access Dashboard

After successful login:
1. ✅ You'll see your account statistics
2. 🚀 Click **"Go to Dashboard"** button
3. 📊 Welcome to your Campaign Management Interface!

---

## 📢 STEP 3: Create Your First Campaign

### **Navigate to Create Campaign Tab:**
1. 📝 Click **"➕ Create Campaign"** tab
2. 📋 Fill the campaign form:

   **Campaign Details:**
   - **Campaign Name:** Give it a descriptive name
   - **Max Groups:** How many groups to post to (limited by your plan)
   
   **Message Content:**
   - **Message:** Your post content (supports emojis and line breaks)
   
   **Timing Settings:**
   - **Min Delay:** Minimum seconds between posts (default: 10)
   - **Max Delay:** Maximum seconds between posts (default: 60)
   
   **Target Groups:**
   - **Group URLs:** One URL per line, like:
     ```
     https://www.facebook.com/groups/example1/
     https://www.facebook.com/groups/example2/
     https://www.facebook.com/groups/example3/
     ```

3. ✅ Click **"Create Campaign"**

---

## ⚡ STEP 4: Start Your Campaign

1. 📢 Go to **"📢 Campaigns"** tab
2. 👀 Find your campaign in the list
3. 🚀 Click **"Start Campaign"** button (only visible for draft campaigns)
4. ✅ Campaign status changes to "running"

---

## 🎉 CONGRATULATIONS!

You're now ready to automate your Facebook group posting! 🚀📢
